# Chittagong Mezban Beef Recipe

## Ingredients
- 1 kg beef
- 3 onions, chopped
- 2 tablespoons ginger-garlic paste
- 1 teaspoon turmeric powder
- 2 teaspoons chili powder
- Salt to taste
- Cooking oil as needed
- Water as needed

## Instructions
1. Wash and clean the beef.
2. Heat oil and cook the chopped onions until golden.
3. Add ginger-garlic paste and spices.
4. Add beef and mix well.
5. Add water and cook until the beef becomes tender.
6. Adjust salt and spices as needed.
7. Serve hot with rice.
