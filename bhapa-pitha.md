# Nanu's Bhapa Pitha Recipe

## Ingredients
- 2 cups rice flour
- 1 cup grated coconut
- 1.5 cups date palm jaggery (gur)
- A pinch of salt
- Water as needed

## Instructions
1. Mix rice flour with a little water and salt.
2. Prepare grated coconut mixed with date palm jaggery.
3. Put the rice flour mixture into a pitha mold.
4. Add the coconut and jaggery filling.
5. Cover with more rice flour.
6. Steam until fully cooked.
7. Serve warm.
