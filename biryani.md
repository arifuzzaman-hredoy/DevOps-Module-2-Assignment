# Nanu's Special Biryani

## Ingredients
- 2 cups basmati rice
- 500g chicken
- 2 onions
- 1 cup yogurt
- 2 tablespoons biryani masala
- Ginger-garlic paste
- Salt to taste
- Cooking oil
- Water as needed

## Instructions
1. Wash and soak the basmati rice.
2. Marinate the chicken with yogurt, spices, and ginger-garlic paste.
3. Fry the onions until golden brown.
4. Cook the marinated chicken until tender.
5. Boil the rice until partially cooked.
6. Layer the rice and chicken in a pot.
7. Cover and cook on low heat until fully done.
8. Serve hot.
