# Nanu's Halua Recipe

## Ingredients
- 1 cup semolina
- 1/2 cup sugar
- 1/4 cup ghee
- Water as needed

## Instructions
1. Heat the ghee in a pan.
2. Add semolina and roast until fragrant.
3. Boil water in a separate pot.
4. Add the roasted semolina slowly while stirring.
5. Add sugar and cook until the mixture thickens.
6. Serve warm.
