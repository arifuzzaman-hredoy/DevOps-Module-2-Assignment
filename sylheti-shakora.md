# Sylheti Shatkora Beef Recipe

## Ingredients
- 500 g beef
- 2 tablespoons shatkora
- 2 onions, sliced
- 1 tablespoon ginger-garlic paste
- Turmeric powder
- Chili powder
- Salt to taste
- Cooking oil
- Water as needed

## Instructions
1. Heat oil and fry the sliced onions.
2. Add ginger-garlic paste and spices.
3. Add beef and cook until browned.
4. Add water and simmer until the beef is tender.
5. Add shatkora and cook until its flavor blends with the curry.
6. Adjust salt and spices.
7. Serve hot with rice.
