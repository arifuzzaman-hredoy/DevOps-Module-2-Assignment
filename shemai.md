# Nanu's Special Shemai

## Ingredients
- 100g shemai (vermicelli)
- 1 litre milk
- 4 tablespoons sugar
- 2 tablespoons ghee
- A few raisins
- A few cashews
- Cardamom to taste

## Instructions
1. Heat ghee in a pan.
2. Lightly fry the shemai until golden.
3. Boil milk in a separate pot.
4. Add the fried shemai to the milk.
5. Add sugar and cardamom.
6. Cook until the shemai becomes soft.
7. Add raisins and cashews.
8. Serve warm or chilled.
